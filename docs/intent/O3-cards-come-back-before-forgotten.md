# O3 Cards come back for study around when they would be forgotten

Inferred on 2026-11-15 from "Anki": spaced repetition is what makes a study app Anki-like.

## Changes

Which cards a study session offers: those due, sooner after a miss and later after a hit.

## Known by

After a correct answer a card is not due again for at least a day, the interval grows with each correct answer given when the card was due, up to a year, and a wrong answer makes it due at once.
