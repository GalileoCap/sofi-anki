# O7 A person can go back and forth between screens and keep a deck's address

From the owner on 2026-11-15: "I want to be able to navigate back and forth in the app or bookmark specific decks... It doesn't need to be a single page any more". Approved by the asking.

## Changes

How a person moves through the app: the browser's back and forward buttons, a phone's back gesture, a bookmark, and a reload.

## Known by

Back and forward step through the screens a person opened, a reload stays on the screen it was on, and a bookmarked deck opens on that deck.
