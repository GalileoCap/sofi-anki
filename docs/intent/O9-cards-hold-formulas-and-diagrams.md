# O9 A card can hold formulas and diagrams

From the owner on 2026-11-15: "Formatting: Latex and Mermaid". Approved by the asking.

## Changes

What a card's text can show: a formula typeset as mathematics, and a diagram drawn from its description.

## Known by

A card with a LaTeX formula or a Mermaid diagram shows it typeset or drawn wherever the card shows, and a card written before shows as it did.
