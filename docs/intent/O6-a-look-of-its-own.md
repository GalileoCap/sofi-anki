# O6 The app looks made for its people, the same on every screen, and is easy to use

From the owner on 2026-11-15, on the first restyle mocks: "They are all extremely AI-looking. Please research ways to help you design without falling to the common AI style(s) and pitfalls (like inconsistency and bad UX)". The tools that serve it are proposed in [docs/design-research.md](../design-research.md), and this project is their trial before session-kit takes them. Approved by the owner on 2026-11-15: "O6 approved".

## Changes

How the app looks and reads to the friends who use it: a look chosen for them rather than the generic one, one set of rules across every screen, and flows that work on the devices they study on.

## Known by

The owner picks a direction that does not read as machine-made, the static design gate passes with no exception left unexplained, and every task in the design brief is driven on a phone width and a desktop width without a step that confuses.
