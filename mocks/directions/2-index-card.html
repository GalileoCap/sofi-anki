Direction 2 of 3: the index card

All three123

The bet: the card is the app. Opening it on a phone shows the next due card, from whichever deck it is in, and a grade brings the next one. Decks, stats and making cards sit one tap behind it.

The one bold element is the card: ruled card stock at the 5 by 3 shape of an index card, its head rule in the deck's own colour, the deck named in its corner. Grades are a teacher's tick, half tick and cross, drawn as marks, so the shape carries the meaning and the colour repeats it.

What it changes: task 1 has no steps before the first card. Grading moves on by itself, so the Next button goes (one step where there are two today). The answer and its code read left-aligned on the card's lines. On a laptop the card keeps its shape in the middle, with the queue and the decks ranked by due beside it, and keys for each grade.

References: the index card, a teacher's marking, school notebook covers for each deck's colour.
