Direction 3 of 4: the reading room

All four1234

The bet: everything is on one quiet page, driven by the keyboard. Home is a ledger of decks, and a deck opens in place into its cards, so making, checking and studying never leave the page.

The one bold element is the date-due stamp: when each card and deck comes back, stamped in ink in the family's monospace, the way a library book carries its due date. Today's stamps are solid, near ones outlined, far ones quiet, so the column reads as a ranking before any number is read.

What it changes: the laptop tasks (2, 3, 5) happen in one place, with keys for every frequent step (j and k to move, Enter to open, s to study what is due, n for a new card). Rare actions sit in one menu per row. On a phone the ledger keeps its order, folds its columns, and keeps one Study button under the thumb, so task 1 is one tap.

References: a library reading room, the date-due slip, Anki's own density as the baseline friends know.
