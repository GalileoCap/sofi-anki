Three directions for sofi-anki

Each direction is a different answer to one question: what do you see when you open the app, and how do you get to studying? Below, the same two moments on a phone, for each: opening the app, and grading the same card. The palette and type differ too, but the arrangement is what you are choosing.

1. The Leitner box

You open on your schedule, drawn as a box: every card from every deck, sorted into today, tomorrow, this week. Study takes what is due today. Each grade says where the card goes next.

2. The index card

You open straight on the next due card. There is no home screen to read first: decks, stats and editing sit behind a button. A grade brings the next card at once.

Opening the app

Grading a card

3. The reading room

You open on a table of your decks, and a deck opens in place into its cards with their due dates. Studying happens inside that same table, one row at a time. Built for the laptop and the keyboard.
