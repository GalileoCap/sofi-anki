Four directions for sofi-anki

Each direction is a different answer to one question: what do you see when you open the app, and how do you get to studying? Below, the same two screens on a phone for each: home, and studying a due card with its answer shown. The card is the same in all four.

1. The Leitner box

Home is your schedule drawn as a box: every card from every deck, sorted into today, tomorrow, this week. Each grade says where the card goes next.

Home

Study, the one screen: a due card, answer shown

2. The index card

The app opens straight on the next due card. Home is behind its Decks button: each deck as an index card. Grades are a tick, half tick and cross.

Home

Study, the one screen: a due card, answer shown

3. The reading room

Home is a table of decks; a deck opens in place into its cards with their due dates. Studying happens in that same table, one row at a time. Laptop first.

Home

Study, the one screen: a due card, answer shown

4. The sticker chart (cutesy)

Home is today's sticker sheet: the daily goal as twenty spots, filled by each card studied in its deck's colour. Decks are notebook covers. Grades are round rubber stamps.
