Three directions for sofi-anki

T7 of the design trial (docs/design-research.md), drawn from the approved brief (docs/design/brief.md) and inside the token roles of docs/design/system.md. Each direction is a different structure for the app, with its own palette and type to match. Pick one, or take a part of one into another, and it becomes the full mock. Every figure and card is the demo decks' own.

DirectionThe betIts bold elementHome on a phone
1. The Leitner boxThe schedule is a box you can see. Studying is its front compartment.The box, each card an edge in its deck's colour, in the compartment of when it comes backThe box, then the decks ranked by due. Study is one tap.
2. The index cardThe card is the app. Opening it shows the next due card.A ruled index card with the deck's colour as its head rule, graded with a teacher's tick, half tick and crossThe next due card. Decks are one tap behind it.
3. The reading roomOne quiet page, driven by the keyboard. Decks open in place.The date-due stamp on every deck and cardThe ledger, folded to two columns. Study is one tap.
